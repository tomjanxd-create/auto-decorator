# Auto Decorator

Decorate a whole level in one click.

Open the editor, press **Esc**, and click **Auto Decorate**. Then pick one of 15 styles:

Hell · Modern · Neon · Nature · Frozen · Ocean · Space · Classic · Cyber · Desert · Candy · Spooky · Minimal · Synthwave · Toxic

Each style:
- adds decoration on top of blocks, hanging under ceilings, and along the ground
- adds large background art across the level
- (optional) sets the background, ground, and start colors

Decoration objects have **no hitboxes**, so they never change gameplay. They are all put in **group 9999** and use color channels **990–993**. That way you can remove them with the *Remove Deco* button or re-roll with another style.
